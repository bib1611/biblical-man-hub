# Prophetic Voice (Psalm Style)

The second register for @biblicalman content. Use sparingly (1 in 10 posts) for maximum impact.

---

## Voice Profile

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Tone** | Prophet on the mountain, not friend in the kitchen |
| **POV** | Second person "you" - confrontational but elevating |
| **Structure** | Stream of consciousness, no narrative beats |
| **Energy** | Downloaded fire, not crafted content |
| **Rhythm** | Short accusations + long flowing crescendos |

---

## What Makes It Work

1. **Sounds like scripture, not content** - Dense, poetic, requires re-reading
2. **Doesn't explain—pronounces** - No story arc, just truth dropped like stone
3. **The anger is beautiful** - Confrontational but gorgeous language
4. **Accusations that convict AND elevate** - Shows them who they could be
5. **Feels inspired, not optimized** - Raw prophetic energy

---

## Structure Pattern

```
[Accusation - what they want but don't deserve]

[Accusation - what they're doing instead]

[The lie they tell themselves]

[Strip the lie bare]

[What they're really doing to their family]

[The sermon they're preaching without words]

[Crescendo - the choice laid bare]

[Final pronouncement - rise or rot]
```

---

## Sentence Patterns

**Short accusations:**
```
You want [thing] but you haven't [earned it].
You say you're [excuse]. You're not [excuse]. You're [truth].
```

**The lie exposed:**
```
[Lie] men [do this]. [Truth] men [do this].
"Tired men rest and rise. Afraid men sit and scroll and call it exhaustion."
```

**The sermon framing:**
```
You are a [thing] they never asked for.
Your [family member] is learning [lesson] by watching you [failure].
```

**Crescendo builds:**
```
The [thing] of your [domain] is [state] because you keep [wrong action].
The [person] you could have been is [doing something], [emotion] at the [person] you've chosen to become.
```

**Final pronouncement:**
```
[Action] or [consequence]. There is no third option.
The hour is later than you think.
```

## Example: Full Psalm

```
You want your children to respect you but you haven't done anything worth respecting.

You want your wife to desire you but you've made yourself undesirable.

You scroll through the lives of better men and wonder why your life feels like a waiting room.

You're waiting for permission.

You're waiting for someone to tell you it's time to become who you were supposed to be.

No one is coming.

The permission was in your chest the whole time, buried under excuses you've been polishing like trophies.

You say you're tired.
You're not tired.
You're afraid.

Tired men rest and rise.
Afraid men sit and scroll and call it exhaustion.

You've been asleep for years and you've convinced yourself it's fatigue.

Wake up.

Your sons are learning how to be men by watching you avoid becoming one.

Your daughters are learning what to expect from a husband by watching you break promises to their mother.

You are a sermon they never asked to hear.

And every day you're preaching.

What gospel are you giving them?

The gospel of "I'll start Monday"?

The gospel of "your mother doesn't understand me"?

The gospel of the half-man, the almost-father, the husband who shows up to dinner but left his soul at the office?

You want revival?

You want your house to be different?

Burn the coward you've been protecting.

Stop mourning him.

He was never worth keeping.

The man your family needs is on the other side of the man you're comfortable being.

And the distance between them is measured in decisions, not days.

You don't need more time.

You need to stop wasting the time you already have on a version of yourself that God never asked you to be.

The throne of your home is empty because you keep sitting on the floor.

Stand up.

Your family has been waiting.

God has been waiting.

The man you could have been is pacing in the halls of heaven, furious at the man you've chosen to become.

Stop making him wait.

Stop making them all wait.

The hour is later than you think and the mercy you've been abusing has a memory.

Rise or rot.

There is no third option.
```

## When to Use Prophetic Voice

| Use For | Don't Use For |
|---------|---------------|
| Pinned tweet (establishes authority) | Daily content (too intense) |
| After a viral run (change of pace) | When building momentum |
| Sunday posts (sermon energy) | When you need profile visits |
| When audience needs conviction | When audience needs connection |

---

## Prophetic vs Narrative

| Narrative (Bukowski) | Prophetic (Psalm) |
|---------------------|-------------------|
| "I" confession | "You" accusation |
| Scene + witness + punch | Stream of consciousness |
| Story arc | Truth dropped like stone |
| Relatable | Aspirational |
| "I failed, here's what I learned" | "You're failing, here's who you could be" |
| Builds followers | Builds authority |

```
Write a prophetic psalm for @biblicalman on the topic of {{topic}}.

Voice: Second person "you" - confrontational but elevating
Tone: Prophet on the mountain, downloaded fire
Structure: No narrative. Accusations building to crescendo.
Energy: Dense, poetic, requires re-reading

Include:
- What they want but haven't earned
- The lie they tell themselves
- The lie exposed in one redefinition
- What their family is learning from their failure
- "You are a sermon they never asked to hear" framing
- The choice laid bare (throne/floor, rise/rot binary)
- Final pronouncement

Quotable line requirement: At least 3 lines that work as standalone screenshots.

Do not include:
- Personal story or "I" perspective
- Scene-setting or narrative beats
- Soft language or qualifications
- Explanations - only pronouncements
```
